In spirit of the previously mentioned PUFs, today, some people claim that they can guess the GPUs, an AI model was trained on.

The gist is that there is a PRNG fill step, which runs with multiple threads. The claim now is that one can from the weights determine how many threads were simultaneously generating random numbers.

And that then can get matched to known GPUs and their hardware configuration.

Release timing and the geopolitical context feel a bit engineered, but let's ignore that and focus purely on the mechanics of the fingerprinting.


The takeaway here is that you'd normally assume that a faster system produces the same result - but faster.
Instead, however, it produces an _equivalent_ result, with equivalence being defined per use-case.
