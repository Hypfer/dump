The Wikimedia Commons host (among other interesting things) this PDF that is a part of the NTSB's report on China Eastern Airlines Flight 5735,
which is, for the purposes of this text, a plane that crashed in 2022.

Specifically, it's about the damaged flight data and voice recorders that were handed to a data recovery team, and said team documents (with pictures!) what they did to do so.
