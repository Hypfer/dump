So there's this.. uh.. group? entity? called cyberleek, which has been doing cryptocurrency-fueled cybercrimes and evidently having some inside access to Rockstar Games and/or their supply chain.
And that's IT boulevard news.

However, what I think is noteworthy here is that they seem to be using LLM writing for all of the public communication I saw.

Which is interesting, because doing that pretty much completely rules out anyone being able to track you purely by your writing style.
Kinda like writing threatening letters using words cut from newspapers but less artsy, less recyclable and with less fingerprints or other DNA on it.
So the biggest bug with LLM writing (there being no person's unique voice) can be a feature if the surrounding constraints and goals change.

I would suspect to see similar lack of personal fingerprints in how LLMs traverse and hack systems.
I think I remember once reading about people getting identified by how they chain their commands and which order of CLI flags they use?

Of course, arguably, this is (depending on who you ask) old news by now, but what isn't?
